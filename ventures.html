Venture 2 · NGO & Social Initiative

Fraud Free Bharat

Empowering India to recognise, resist, and report online fraud.

Our Mission

Fraud Free Bharat is a non-profit initiative I founded after witnessing ordinary people — students, homemakers, senior citizens and job-seekers — lose their hard-earned savings to online scams, network marketing frauds, and digital deception.

Through workshops, social media awareness campaigns, one-on-one guidance, and community outreach, we educate people about the tactics fraudsters use and teach them how to stay safe in the digital world.

What We Fight

  • Network marketing (MLM) and pyramid scheme fraud
  • Job offer and work-from-home scams
  • Investment and Ponzi scheme frauds
  • KYC update, OTP, and banking fraud
  • Social media impersonation and romance scams
  • Loan app harassment and digital lending fraud

Our Impact

6+ Years Active
1,000+ People Educated
200+ Scams Exposed

3 Rules to Stay Safe

01

Never share OTP, Aadhaar, or bank PIN — no legitimate company will ever ask for it.

02

Verify before you trust — check the company's registration, SEBI/RBI approval, and reviews.

03

Report immediately — call 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in.
