    PUBLIC SAFETY ADVISORY // 6 YEARS RESEARCH

    4 Red Flags of a Network Marketing / Cyber Scam

    Before transferring your hard-earned money to any online business or recruitment scheme, check these 4 critical warning signs:

    RED FLAG 01 Upfront "Joining Kits"

    They force you to buy an expensive starter kit, training course, or crypto package to become a "distributor".

    RED FLAG 02 Recruitment Over Retail

    Over 80% of income comes from adding downlines (new members), not from selling products to outside customers.

    RED FLAG 03 Guaranteed Daily ROI

    Promises of 1% to 5% daily returns from "AI Trading", "Forex bots", or "Mining pools". Real markets have no guaranteed returns.

    RED FLAG 04 Personal UPI Transfers

    They ask you to transfer funds to personal UPI IDs or download unauthorized APK apps not vetted by Google Play.
