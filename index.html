🛡️ Social Impact & Safety

The Fraud Buster Mission

Online deception, fake job schemes, and investment frauds ruin livelihoods every day. As an active cyber safety advocate, I equip citizens with practical knowledge to detect and prevent digital fraud before damage occurs.

1

Never Pay for "Job Verification"

Legitimate recruiters never ask for registration fees, training deposits, or task guarantees before employment.

2

Question Guaranteed Returns

Any WhatsApp or Telegram group promising guaranteed daily payouts or crypto doubling is 100% a fraudulent Ponzi scam.

3

Act Within Golden Hours (1930)

If defrauded, immediately dial India’s national cybercrime helpline 1930 and report on cybercrime.gov.in.
