About Me

Continuous Learning, Practical Problem-Solving, and Building Useful Solutions

My professional journey began in 2019 when I started working in Gurugram. That initial experience gave me a solid grounding in organizational workflows and real-world technology operations.

After gaining industry experience, I moved into freelancing, taking on a wide variety of digital projects for clients and individuals. Over the years, I learned to adapt quickly across different technical environments, tackle unforeseen digital challenges, and deliver functional solutions through hands-on work.

Through this work, I naturally developed practical experience in cybersecurity and digital technologies. Working closely with people revealed how vulnerable everyday internet users and small businesses are to digital threats, which led me to actively help people navigate technical hurdles and protect themselves online.

Today, as AI models and tools are rapidly reshaping the digital landscape, I am actively expanding my foundation into new areas. I don't claim to know everything about AI; instead, I approach it as an experienced digital practitioner who loves to learn, experiment openly, and build useful solutions as technology evolves.
