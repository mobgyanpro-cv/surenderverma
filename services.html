Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about coaching, business consulting, and fraud advice.

Who is your career coaching primarily for?

My coaching is suited for students entering the job market, early-to-mid career professionals seeking promotions or career pivots, and individuals wanting to leverage AI tools to stand out.

What does Growth Karo specifically help business owners with?

Growth Karo focuses on online positioning, customer acquisition strategies, and high-conversion content. We help small business owners cut through noise and attract qualified inquiries.

What should I do if I think I've been contacted by a scammer?

Do not send any money or OTPs. Capture screenshots of all chat logs, numbers, and transaction IDs. Visit my Fraud Awareness page for immediate safety protocols or reach out directly on WhatsApp.

How do we communicate during sessions?

Consultations and coaching sessions are conducted via Google Meet, Zoom, or direct phone/WhatsApp calls, depending on what is most convenient for you.
