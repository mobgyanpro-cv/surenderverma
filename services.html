Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding working together on freelance projects.

What types of web projects do you take on?

I build fast, responsive personal portfolios, landing pages, business websites, and prototype interfaces using modern vanilla web technologies and lightweight toolsets.

How do you integrate AI tools into content and development?

I use AI models and tools to augment production speed — from exploratory scripting and automated video editing workflows to rapid prototyping and problem-solving — always accompanied by human curation and quality control.
