Fraud Awareness & Safety Guide

Led by Surender Verma with 6+ years on the ground, having resolved 700+ network marketing (MLM) cases and 100+ mobile & online money fraud cases across India.

🚨

Been Defrauded Recently? Act Within Golden Hours

If you lost money to an online scam in India, immediately call the National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 (formerly 155260) and log a formal complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Fast reporting enables banks to freeze fraud accounts before money is withdrawn.

Threat Breakdown

Common Online Scams in India

Scammers continually innovate, but their underlying psychological traps follow predictable patterns.

💼

Fake Part-Time Job & Task Scams

Victims receive unsolicited WhatsApp/Telegram offers promising ₹2,000–₹5,000/day for liking YouTube videos or rating hotels. After a tiny initial payout, victims are tricked into "recharge tasks" costing lakhs.

📈

Crypto & High-Return Ponzi Apps

Fraudulent trading apps show falsified high returns on fake dashboards. When you attempt to withdraw, scammers demand "withdrawal tax" or "margin fees" until the victim's funds are completely drained.

🕸️

Predatory Network Marketing (MLM)

Schemes disguising chain-recruitment as legitimate businesses. Focus is placed on recruiting downstream members who pay entry fees for overpriced products rather than genuine market customer sales.

👮

"Digital Arrest" & Impersonation Scams

Scammers pose as CBI, Police, Customs, or TRAI officers, claiming a parcel containing contraband or illegal SIM cards was seized in your name. They intimidate victims into transferring money for "verification."

Detection

Critical Warning Signs (Red Flags)

If you encounter any of these triggers, disengage immediately.

!

Artificial Urgency & Fear Tactics

Pressure tactics like "Act in 10 minutes or your account will be blocked" or "Police is on the way" are designed to bypass logical thinking.

!

Requests to Download Screen-Sharing Apps

Never install AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or RustDesk on the instruction of someone claiming to be bank or customer support. They read OTPs directly from your screen.

!

Payment Demanded via UPI to Personal Accounts

Legitimate organizations and government departments never request money transfers to private individual UPI IDs or unknown mule accounts.

Emergency Action Plan

What to Do If You Suspect Fraud

1. Stop All Further Transfers

No matter what the fraudster promises (e.g. "Send ₹10,000 more to unlock your whole balance"), NEVER send another rupee. The promised balance does not exist.

2. Contact Your Bank Immediately

Call your bank's 24x7 customer care number to freeze the compromised account, UPI, and debit cards. Request a transaction dispute token.

3. Preserve Every Piece of Evidence

Take screenshots of chat histories, transaction reference numbers (UTR/RRN), recipient UPI IDs, email headers, and phone call logs before scammers delete them.

4. Report to National Cyber Crime Portal

Register a complaint on cybercrime.gov.in or dial 1930. Keep your Acknowledgement Number safely for police follow-up.
