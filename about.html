About Surender

Behind the Mission

A founder, career coach, and digital defender dedicated to genuine empowerment, online safety, and ethical growth.

📍 Rooted in Sirsa, Haryana, India

Who I Am & What Drives Me

My journey is rooted in a simple conviction: people deserve to grow their careers and build businesses without being exploited by online deception or predatory schemes.

As the Founder & CEO of Growth Karo, I help entrepreneurs and small business owners craft authoritative personal brands, streamline their digital outreach, and create real engagement. At the same time, I mentor students and working professionals, guiding them through career transitions and modern workplace readiness.

Witnessing firsthand the devastating financial and emotional toll of fake investment apps, MLM frauds, and job scams, I committed myself to becoming a "Fraud Buster". I conduct proactive awareness drives, educate citizens on defensive digital practices, and assist victims in utilizing India’s official cyber reporting mechanisms.
