Who I Am & How I Work

My professional journey began in 2019 when I started working in Gurugram. That early corporate and operational exposure gave me a disciplined foundation and an appreciation for how technology underpins modern workflows.

After gaining hands-on professional experience, I transitioned into freelancing and independent digital projects. Working directly with clients and individuals across diverse requirements taught me how to solve real problems under real constraints. Over the years, I developed practical experience in cybersecurity and digital safety, having personally handled 700+ network marketing (MLM) fraud cases, performed 100+ mobile hacking, Trojan, and malware cleanups, and intervened in 70+ unauthorized bank debits and credit card fraud cases.

My career has never been static. I continuously learn new skills, explore shifting tools, and expand my capabilities through direct experimentation. Today, as AI models and tools reshape the digital landscape, I am channeling my practical foundation into AI-assisted content creation, modern website development, experimenting with emerging models, and prototyping small SaaS products.
